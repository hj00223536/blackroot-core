# BLACKROOT (BRT) proposed monetary policy

**Status: owner-approved design direction; not implemented or activated.**

The owner has selected the following proposed allocation for review and implementation:

| Allocation | Amount | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Founder allocation | 20,000,000 BRT | 50% |
| Mining rewards | 20,000,000 BRT | 50% |
| **Total proposed supply** | **40,000,000 BRT** | **100%** |

## Founder allocation

- The intended beneficiary is the project founder.
- The allocation is intended to be spendable, not time-locked.
- No founder wallet address or public key has yet been supplied or safely generated. No private key may be generated by CI or committed to this repository.
- The founder allocation must not be credited to a wallet until a receiving address is generated and verified through a secure wallet process.
- The genesis transaction/output format and coinbase/genesis spend rules must explicitly support this allocation and be covered by independent test vectors.

## Mining allocation

- Exactly 20,000,000 BRT is reserved for block-production rewards over the network's emission schedule.
- The subsidy curve, block interval, halving rules, rounding, final reward, and handling of unclaimed rewards remain to be specified and tested. The previous 50 BRT / 210,000-block schedule must not be reused unchanged because it was designed around an approximately 21 million BRT issuance.
- Consensus validation must enforce the mining allocation cap; miners must not be able to create more than the permitted cumulative reward.

## Liquidity and launch constraints

- No cash liquidity pool is being funded or promised by the founder at this stage.
- A local development/test network can be used to test issuance and transfers without claiming market liquidity or monetary value.
- Wallet balances are not cash. A spendable founder allocation does not guarantee buyers, an exchange listing, a market price, or the ability to convert BRT to Iraqi dinars or another currency.
- Do not solicit deposits, sell BRT, advertise a guaranteed price, or describe the network as launched.
- Any later public testnet/mainnet requires the launch gates, independent consensus/security review, operational readiness, and qualified Iraqi legal advice described in the launch plan.

## Required implementation sequence

1. Freeze a complete monetary specification for the 40,000,000 BRT model.
2. Implement and test the 20,000,000 BRT mining emission cap and exact reward schedule.
3. Design the founder genesis allocation without embedding private keys in source control; verify the receiving address through a secure wallet workflow.
4. Add deterministic genesis vectors and tests proving the founder allocation, spendability policy, mining cap, and total supply.
5. Run cross-version CI and independent consensus-vector review before any network activation.

This document records the selected design direction only. It does not change the current consensus constants, genesis candidate, or any existing wallet balance.
