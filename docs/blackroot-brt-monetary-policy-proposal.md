# BLACKROOT (BRT) monetary policy

**Status: owner-approved allocation and mining schedule; implementation is undergoing CI validation. Network activation is not authorized.**

The owner approved this allocation and fixed mining curve:

| Allocation | Amount | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Founder genesis allocation | 20,000,000 BRT | 50% |
| Mining rewards | 20,000,000 BRT | 50% |
| **Total maximum supply** | **40,000,000 BRT** | **100%** |

## Founder allocation

- The intended beneficiary is the project founder.
- The allocation is intended to be spendable, not time-locked.
- No founder wallet address or public key has yet been safely generated. No private key may be generated by CI or committed to this repository.
- The founder allocation must not be credited to a wallet until a receiving address is generated and independently verified through a secure wallet process.
- The founder output must be included in the genesis transaction and proven spendable by independent transaction vectors. This has not yet been implemented.

## Mining schedule — approved

- Fixed block subsidy: **10 BRT**.
- Eligible heights: **1 through 2,000,000 inclusive**.
- Number of eligible blocks: **2,000,000**.
- Maximum scheduled mining issuance: **20,000,000 BRT**.
- Height 0 has no mining subsidy; founder issuance is a separate genesis allocation.
- Heights above 2,000,000 have zero scheduled subsidy.
- At a 600-second target block interval, 2,000,000 blocks take about 38 years if blocks arrive on schedule.
- Unclaimed subsidy is not carried forward.
- Transaction fees, if enabled, transfer existing BRT and must not increase the supply cap.

The selected schedule is now the reference subsidy used by `src/blackroot_consensus.py`; CI is validating the change. The reference block validator limits each coinbase to the scheduled subsidy plus fees. Full consensus and supply-accounting review remains required.

## Liquidity and launch constraints

- No cash liquidity pool is funded or promised by the founder at this stage.
- A local development/test network can test issuance and transfers without claiming market liquidity or monetary value.
- Wallet balances are not cash. A spendable founder allocation does not guarantee buyers, an exchange listing, a market price, or conversion to Iraqi dinars or another currency.
- Do not solicit deposits, sell BRT, advertise a guaranteed price, or describe the network as launched.
- Any later public testnet/mainnet requires the launch gates, independent consensus/security review, operational readiness, and qualified Iraqi legal advice described in the launch plan.
